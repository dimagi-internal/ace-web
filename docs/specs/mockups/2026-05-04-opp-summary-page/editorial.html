How long should the FLW spend on the dietary recall section?
About 4–5 minutes. The PDD's Evidence Model expects at least 3 of the 7 prompts answered with a specific food example to score the section as complete. — from FLW training guide §2.3
What if the photo is blurry?
Retake. The Deliver app rejects submissions where the GPS is > 30m off and flags blurry photos for LLO review the same evening. — from Quick reference card
